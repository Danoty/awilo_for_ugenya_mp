WILLIS OUMA ONDIEK — UGENYA 2027 VISION

UGENYA MANYIEN
A UNITED, EDUCATED, HEALTHY AND ECONOMICALLY EMPOWERED UGENYA

Willis Ouma Ondiek is a father, finance expert, divine teacher and community-minded leader seeking to serve as Member of Parliament for Ugenya Constituency in the 2027 General Election.

CORE DEVELOPMENT AGENDA

EDUCATION
Stronger bursary and scholarship access; support for vulnerable learners; improved school infrastructure; digital learning; support for teachers; and wider pathways to university, TVET, technical training or entrepreneurship.

HEALTHCARE
Advocacy for properly equipped facilities, adequate medical supplies, reliable staffing, maternal and child healthcare, preventive programmes and stronger access to national health insurance.

JOBS AND ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT
Support for SMEs, cooperatives, agriculture, value addition, vocational skills, digital opportunities and inclusive enterprise for young people, women and persons living with disabilities.

INFRASTRUCTURE
Improved infrastructure that connects communities with essential services, markets and economic opportunity.

COMMUNITY UNITY
Dialogue, inclusion and shared responsibility across political, clan and other divisions—bringing Ugenya together around a common development agenda.

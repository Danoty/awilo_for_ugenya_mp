How to verify this website, transfer safely and report suspicious activity.

Verify before paying

  • Use only willisondiekfoundation.org.
  • The authorised KCB details published here are: Willis Ondiek, account 1250748712, Bondo Branch.
  • Foundation references begin with FND; campaign references begin with CMP.
  • Confirm unexpected instructions through the published email or WhatsApp number.
  • Never disclose a banking password, PIN, card number or one-time code.

What this website does not do

The site does not collect card details, online-banking credentials, identity documents or payment authentication codes. Direct transfers are completed in the donor’s own bank application, official website or branch.

Responsible disclosure

Report suspected website vulnerabilities, impersonation or fraudulent payment instructions to info@willisondiekfoundation.org. Do not include passwords, PINs or unrelated personal data.

Payment disputes

Contact your bank immediately if you believe a transfer was unauthorised. Provide the transfer reference and notify the Foundation or campaign through verified channels.

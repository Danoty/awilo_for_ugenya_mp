Dignity and protection

Safeguarding commitments.

Foundation and campaign activities should protect children, persons with disabilities and adults at risk from abuse, exploitation, harassment and avoidable exposure.

Our commitments

  • Obtain informed permission before publishing identifiable photographs or stories.
  • Never publish a child’s full identity, school, medical information or precise location without a lawful safeguarding reason and guardian approval.
  • Use respectful language and avoid presenting beneficiaries as campaign endorsements.
  • Require appropriate supervision, conduct standards and reporting routes for staff and volunteers.
  • Respond promptly to safety concerns and refer emergencies to appropriate authorities.

Reporting

A named safeguarding lead and confidential reporting channel must be formally approved before public programme registration begins. Until then, use info@willisondiekfoundation.org for non-emergency concerns and contact emergency services where immediate danger exists.

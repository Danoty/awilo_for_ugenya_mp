THE SOCIAL CONTRACT

Leadership that listens, plans and delivers.

Development should not be measured by speeches, but by the quality of life enjoyed by ordinary citizens.

Ugenya Manyien is a commitment to servant leadership, accountability and inclusive development—ensuring that every village, household and generation benefits from meaningful and sustainable progress.

THE DEVELOPMENT AGENDA

Eight commitments.
One Ugenya.

01Water for every household02Healthcare with dignity03Education for the future04Protecting the vulnerable05Agriculture and wealth06Youth and employment07Women empowerment08Infrastructure and digital access
01WATER

Water for every household.

No family should walk long distances in search of safe drinking water.

  • Construct strategic boreholes and expand community water and piped systems.
  • Rehabilitate existing infrastructure and promote rainwater harvesting.
  • Protect rivers, springs and wetlands through community conservation.
  • Establish sustainable community water-management committees.
  • Support irrigation that increases agricultural productivity.
GOAL: Clean, safe and affordable water for every household.
02HEALTH

Quality healthcare with dignity.

Healthcare is a right, not a privilege.

  • Improve and equip facilities, essential medicines and reliable staffing.
  • Strengthen maternal, child, emergency and ambulance services.
  • Train and facilitate Community Health Volunteers.
  • Advance prevention, sanitation, mental health and early screening.
  • Advocate for better conditions for healthcare workers.
VISION: Every resident receives quality care with dignity.
03EDUCATION

Education for the future.

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, develop talent and prepare for a productive future.

  • Improve classrooms, laboratories, sanitation and learning environments.
  • Expand bursaries, TVET institutions, digital literacy and mentorship.
  • Build community digital libraries and modern public learning resources.
  • Connect education with employment, entrepreneurship and industrial skills.
  • Champion a public university in Ugenya through the relevant national institutions.
BELIEF: Higher education is an investment in our economy and people.
04DIGNITY

Protecting the vulnerable.

A caring society protects those who need support the most.

  • Help teenage mothers return to school and sponsor vulnerable learners.
  • Support orphans, children, senior citizens and persons with disabilities.
  • Build partnerships with churches, NGOs and community organisations.
  • Provide counselling, family support, skills and economic empowerment.
PROMISE: No one should be left behind.
05AGRICULTURE

The heart of Ugenya.

Empower the farmer, strengthen households and build a food-secure constituency.

  • Improve access to inputs, extension services and climate-smart training.
  • Promote irrigation, mechanisation, value addition and agro-processing.
  • Strengthen cooperatives, livestock, fisheries and agricultural credit.
  • Connect farmers to markets, technology and development programmes.
VISION: Agriculture that creates sustainable local wealth.
06YOUTH

Employment and entrepreneurship.

Young people deserve opportunities to work, innovate and build businesses.

  • Establish innovation and entrepreneurship centres.
  • Improve access to business finance and vocational skills.
  • Create internship, apprenticeship and digital-work pathways.
  • Support sport, talent development and ICT innovation.
07WOMEN

Women empowerment.

Women are the foundation of our families and communities.

  • Strengthen enterprise, savings and investment programmes.
  • Support maternal healthcare, leadership and value-addition businesses.
  • Protect women against gender-based violence.
08CONNECTION

Infrastructure and digital connectivity.

Development requires strong physical and digital connections.

  • Better roads connecting villages and modern markets for traders.
  • Expanded rural electrification and internet connectivity.
  • Street lighting and improved drainage in trading centres.

A COMMITMENT TO DELIVERY

Every village matters.
Every household counts.

This manifesto is a social contract between leadership and the people of Ugenya. Its centre is the ordinary citizen: the mother seeking water, the patient waiting for medicine, the learner needing fees, the young person seeking work, the farmer building a harvest and the vulnerable child hoping for a brighter future.

Actions, not talk.

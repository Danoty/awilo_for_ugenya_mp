Established 2015 • Our identity

A belief in dignity, opportunity and human potential.

The Foundation is a community development initiative founded by Rev. Father Willis Ondiek to advance social, economic and civic development.

Our story

At the heart of the Foundation is a belief that every person deserves an opportunity to live with dignity and fulfil their potential. Education has remained one of its principal areas of intervention, supported through personal fundraising and community support.

The Foundation’s work also extends to empowerment, social support and faith-based community transformation.

Since 2015, the Foundation story has been guided by a clear belief: lasting support should protect dignity and create opportunity.

VISION

Empowered communities

To build empowered, self-reliant and dignified communities where every person—regardless of circumstance—has an opportunity to thrive.

MISSION

Transforming lives

To use education, economic empowerment, social support and faith-based initiatives to donate to sustainable community development.

PHILOSOPHY

Beyond giving

Charity should create opportunity by equipping people with knowledge, resources and confidence to build better lives.
